# Bandarban Resort Hub

An all-in-one resort discovery, comparison and online booking platform for Bandarban, Bangladesh.

Tourists visiting Bandarban often have to search through many Facebook pages and websites, or contact resorts one by one, to find prices, room photos and availability. Bandarban Resort Hub brings every participating resort into one place, so travelers can find, compare and book the right stay for their budget, room preferences and travel plans.

## Features

- **Resort discovery:** Browse all registered resorts with photos, locations, starting prices, ratings, reviews, facilities and directions.
- **Budget-based filtering:** Filter by price range (Budget, Standard, Premium, Luxury), location, facilities, ratings and room types.
- **Resort comparison:** Compare multiple resorts side by side on price, room quality, views, amenities, location, ratings, food and cancellation policy.
- **Room details and gallery:** View interior photos, balcony and mountain views, room categories, facilities, capacity and availability.
- **Online booking:** Choose a room, select dates, enter guest details, see the total cost, pay an advance and receive a confirmation.
- **Transparent refunds:** The advance payment, cancellation fee and refund amount are shown clearly before payment.
- **Role-based dashboards:** Separate dashboards for tourists, resort owners and administrators.

## User roles

| Role | Capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| Tourist | Search, compare, book, cancel, review and save resorts to a wishlist |
| Resort Owner | Manage resort details, rooms, prices, availability and bookings |
| Administrator | Verify resorts, manage users, bookings, payments and refunds |

## Design principles

Clean, minimal and responsive across mobile, tablet and desktop, with high-quality resort photography, simple navigation, clear booking buttons and accessible colors.

## Roadmap

- AI-based resort recommendations
- Tour packages and travel guides
- Other tourist destinations in Bangladesh
- Multi-language support (Bangla and English)
- Personalized travel planning
- Verified tourist reviews

## Data note

Resort listings, prices, photos and availability are meant to be verified and maintained by participating resort owners. Real-time availability and refund processing need the backend and payment gateway integration.

## Vision

To become the most trusted booking platform for Bandarban, making resort discovery, comparison and reservation easier, more transparent and more convenient for everyone.
